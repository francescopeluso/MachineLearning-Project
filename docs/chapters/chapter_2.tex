\section{Background: dense prediction and instance metrics}
\label{sec:background}

\subsection{Dense prediction}

A classification CNN answers one question per image: its pooling stages
progressively discard \emph{where} things are in order to decide \emph{what} the
image is. Segmentation asks the opposite -- one decision per pixel -- so the
spatial detail the encoder throws away has to be rebuilt. A decoder of transposed
convolutions can upsample the bottleneck back to full resolution, but for
structures a few pixels wide that is not enough: by the bottleneck a thin filament
has already been pooled out of existence, and no upsampling reinvents it. The
\textbf{U-Net}~\cite{unet} solves exactly this with skip connections that copy each
encoder stage into the decoder stage of the same resolution: the fine detail
travels \emph{around} the bottleneck instead of through it. For dark, thin,
elongated filaments those skips are not a refinement -- they are why the prediction
exists at all.

The encoder need not be trained from scratch. Residual networks~\cite{resnet}
pre-trained on ImageNet~\cite{imagenet} provide general edge and texture filters,
which matters when the training set is small.

\subsection{Instance metrics}

Two masks $P$ and $G$ are compared by their Intersection over Union or by the
closely related Dice coefficient,
%
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{IoU}(P,G) = \frac{|P \cap G|}{|P \cup G|},
\qquad
\mathrm{Dice}(P,G) = \frac{2\,|P \cap G|}{|P| + |G|},
\end{equation}
%
both pixel-level measures. \textbf{Panoptic Quality}~\cite{panoptic} instead scores
objects: predicted and annotated instances are matched when their IoU exceeds
$0.5$, and
%
\begin{equation}
\mathrm{PQ}
 = \underbrace{\frac{\sum_{\mathrm{TP}} \mathrm{IoU}}{|\mathrm{TP}|}}_{\mathrm{SQ}}
   \times
   \underbrace{\frac{|\mathrm{TP}|}
        {|\mathrm{TP}| + \tfrac{1}{2}|\mathrm{FP}| + \tfrac{1}{2}|\mathrm{FN}|}}_{\mathrm{RQ}},
\end{equation}
%
segmentation quality times recognition quality. The factorisation is what makes PQ
diagnostic: SQ says how well matched masks overlap, RQ how many objects are found
at all. The two metrics disagree in an informative way -- a fragmented or noisy
prediction barely moves Dice and destroys RQ.
